3. Who else handles your data

We use a few services to run the game. They only get what they need to do their job:

  • Supabase (database and servers, USA): stores accounts, saves and chat.
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting): sees your IP address when you load the site, like any website host.
  • Stripe (payments): handles checkout, card details and receipts. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Content networks (cdnjs, jsDelivr, unpkg) and logo services (Parqet, Financial Modeling Prep, CoinGecko): your browser downloads code, fonts, company logos and coin icons from them, so they see your IP address. You can turn company logos off in Settings.

We don't sell or share your personal information for advertising. We only share it if the law requires us to, or to protect players from harm.

4. Children

Anyone can play. When you sign up we ask for your birth year. If you are under 13:

  • you can only chat with ready-made quick chat phrases and emoji. Free typing is turned off, and the server blocks it too;
  • other players also can only use quick chat in direct messages with you;
  • you can't make a clan, write gift notes or buy anything with real money;
  • we only collect what's in the table above. We never ask for contact details.

Parents: you can ask us to show you or delete your child's account at any time through (choose "I'm a parent"). Tell us the username and we'll take care of it.

5. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See and download your data: Settings → Data & about → Download my data.
  • Delete your account and everything linked to it: Settings → Data & about → Delete my account. It happens right away and can't be undone.
  • Fix things: change your display name in the game, or ask us.
  • Ask questions or complain through . We aim to reply within 30 days. If you're in the EU or UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority. California residents have the same rights under the CCPA, and we don't "sell" or "share" personal information as that law defines it.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS, and the database can only be reached through checked server functions. No system is perfect, though, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

7. Storage in your browser

The game saves your progress and settings in your browser's local storage. We don't use tracking cookies. More detail is in the Cookie Policy.

8. International players

Our servers are in the United States. By playing, your data is handled there.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For big changes we'll show a notice in the game.
