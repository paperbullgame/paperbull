Refund Policy

Last updated

The short version
  • Something went wrong, or you didn't mean to buy it? Ask within 14 days and we'll usually refund it.
  • Cancel VIP any time. It stays on until the end of the month you've paid for.
  • Prices are in US dollars. Your bank may add taxes or fees.

1. When you can get a refund

  • It didn't arrive. If you paid and didn't get your item within 24 hours, you'll get a full refund or the item, whichever you prefer.
  • Accidental or unauthorised purchase. For example, a child bought something without permission. Ask within 14 days for a full refund.
  • Anything else within 14 days. If you haven't used much of what you bought (for example you haven't spent most of the coins), we'll usually refund it. When we refund, we take the matching coins or items back out of your account.
  • VIP. If you forgot to cancel, ask within 7 days of the renewal date and we'll refund that month.

Your local consumer law may give you more rights. Nothing here takes those rights away.

2. How to ask

Use and choose "Refund". Include your username, what you bought and the date. We reply within 5 business days. Stripe sends the money back to the card you paid with; banks usually take 5–10 days.

3. Cancelling VIP

VIP renews automatically each month until you cancel. To cancel, open the game and go to Store → VIP → Cancel renewal. It takes one tap. You can also ask us through . You won't be charged again, and VIP stays on until the end of the period you've already paid for.

4. Chargebacks

Please talk to us first, since it's faster. If a payment is reversed through your bank, the items from that purchase are removed.
