Cookie Policy

Last updated

The short version
  • PAPERBULL doesn't use cookies for tracking or ads.
  • The game saves your progress in your browser's local storage, so it works.

1. What we store in your browser

WhatWhyNeeded?
Your game save (pb2.* keys)Fake cash, trades, pets, items and settingsYes, the game can't save without it
Login session (pb2.cloud)Keeps you logged in to your online accountYes, if you use an account
Preferences such as theme, sound, watchlist, alerts and notificationsRemembers your choicesYes
Notice dismissed (pb2.cookieOk)So the storage notice doesn't keep showingYes

None of this is used for advertising, and none of it is shared with advertisers. You can erase all of it with Settings → Data & about → Clear this device, or through your browser's settings.

2. Other websites

When the game loads, your browser also downloads code, fonts, company logos and coin icons from other services: cdnjs, jsDelivr, unpkg, Parqet, Financial Modeling Prep and CoinGecko. If you buy something, you go to Stripe's checkout page. These services see your IP address and may set their own cookies, which their own policies cover. You can turn company logos off in Settings.

3. Analytics

We don't use Google Analytics or any other tracking tool. The only numbers we look at, like how many players are online, come from our own game server.
